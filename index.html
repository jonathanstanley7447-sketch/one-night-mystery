The Missing Minute

At 11:54 PM, every clock in Bellwether House began showing 11:47. One guest remembers the seven minutes that disappeared. They refuse to say why.

Final interview

Only one story points toward orbit.

The decoded word is not a password. It is a motive. Choose the person whose access, timing, and explanation fit all three exhibits.

The fax says ORBIT. Which person admits needing exactly seven minutes to observe something in the sky?
7
Case solved

Mara borrowed seven minutes from tomorrow.

The object above Bellwether House was not a star. It was tomorrow, briefly visible in orbit. Mara stopped the linked clocks at 11:47 and used the brass box as a temporal anchor, creating seven unrecorded minutes in which to observe it.

The fax arrived after midnight because those minutes had to come from somewhere. At dawn, every clock advanced from 5:53 directly to 6:00. The debt was repaid.

The house remembers · The case is closed
